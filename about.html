About AQIB HOSPITAL

Setting benchmarks in tertiary healthcare and clinical safety

Our Story & Legacy

Founded with a vision to make tertiary-level healthcare accessible and reliable, AQIB HOSPITAL has grown into a 350-bed multispecialty medical center equipped with modular operation theaters, dedicated critical care units, and automated clinical laboratories.

Our multidisciplinary teams bring together board-certified consultants, clinical pharmacists, specialized nurses, and rehabilitation therapists working collaboratively around every patient.
